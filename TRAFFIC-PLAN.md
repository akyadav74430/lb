# Lovebite traffic plan

This plan focuses on sustainable organic and referral traffic. It avoids bots,
fake profiles, keyword-stuffed doorway pages, purchased link networks, and
guaranteed-ranking schemes.

## First 48 hours: measurement

1. Add `https://www.lovebite.live` to Google Search Console and verify the DNS
   property.
2. Submit `https://www.lovebite.live/sitemap.xml`.
3. Import the property into Bing Webmaster Tools and submit the same sitemap.
4. Record baseline impressions, clicks, indexed pages, and queries. Track these
   pages first:
   - `/`
   - `/call-girls`
   - `/escorts`
   - `/faq`
   - the city pages that have real approved listings
5. Use a separate UTM source for every legitimate channel, for example:
   `?utm_source=telegram&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=launch`.

## Week 1: make the directory useful in more places

- Publish real, consented listings in the cities with demand: Mumbai, Delhi NCR,
  Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Jaipur, Lucknow, Ahmedabad, and Goa.
- Complete each listing's city, state, district, locality, services, rates,
  photos, and availability fields. Thin or incomplete profiles do not create
  useful search landing pages.
- Ask every companion to share their own profile URL from their public social
  account or Telegram channel. A profile owner is the best source of qualified
  referral traffic.
- Keep only cities with real public listings in the sitemap. Do not create
  pages for empty cities just to target keywords.

## Weeks 2–3: publish linkable safety content

Use `/faq` as the main safety resource and promote it independently of profile
pages. Publish one genuinely useful article per week, linking to the relevant
city pages only where the page has listings:

1. How to spot advance-payment scams on classified directories.
2. What a duplicate-photo check can and cannot prove.
3. A privacy checklist for first meetings and direct contact.
4. How companions can remove a listing or report stolen photographs.

Each article should cite the site's actual process, include a last-updated date,
and avoid claims the site cannot verify. Link to these resources from Telegram,
relevant community discussions, and genuine editorial outreach.

## Weeks 3–4: earn real referrals

- Submit the site to established adult directories that accept independent,
  consent-based listings and disclose the site affiliation.
- Pitch the anti-scam and photo-removal process to regional journalists and
  digital-safety writers. Offer the FAQ as a reference, not as a mass link drop.
- Build a useful Telegram channel with listing updates, safety reminders, and
  removal/reporting information. Share profile URLs only when the profile owner
  has opted in.
- Ask local hospitality, travel, and nightlife publishers for editorial coverage
  of the safety and privacy angle rather than buying links.

## Weekly scorecard

Track:

- Search Console clicks and impressions by city page.
- Indexed versus submitted sitemap URLs.
- Referring domains and referral sessions.
- Telegram link clicks using UTM tags.
- Profile page views and contact-action clicks.
- Reports, removals, and profile-owner opt-outs.

Traffic quality matters more than raw visits. Do not use automated visits or
incentivise clicks; they make the analytics noisy and do not build search demand.
